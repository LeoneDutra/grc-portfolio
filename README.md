# GRC Portfolio

Governance, risk and compliance work samples for a fictional craft brewery ("Kettle & Cipher Brewing"): risk assessment, NIST CSF 2.0 gap analysis, security policies and a business continuity plan.

## Why this repo

Years of HACCP, quality audits, SOPs and lot traceability in brewing map directly to GRC work.

## Roadmap

- [ ] Risk assessment and risk register
- [ ] NIST CSF 2.0 gap analysis
- [ ] Policies: acceptable use, passwords/MFA, incident response
- [ ] BCP for a ransomware-driven production outage
